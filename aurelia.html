The platform in context

A computer. An ecosystem.

In this fictional timeline, Logix launches Aurelia as a complete personal-computing platform: hardware, enhanced CP/M, AION applications, developer programs, support and the Computer Academy.

1978-06-05

NCC 1978 · Aurelia launches with a Z80A and the FlexiBUS peripheral standard.

1979-11

COMDEX, November 1979 · Aurelia+ arrives alongside Pulse and Abacus 2.0. The source specifies the month only.

1980-11

Software direction · Artemis becomes AION’s primary platform; Aurelia receives maintenance and simplified refreshes.

1981-05-04

NCC 1981 · An optional network card and CP/M upgrade bring the Aurelia family into the networked business platform.
