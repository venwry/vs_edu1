Aurelia+ AION memory revision

Aurelia+ now uses eight AION 64K DRAMs. Revised manufacturing costs flow through balanced prices and existing price elasticity; no additional marketing or demand bonus is invented. PRO sales start conditionally in 1981 and add no 1980 revenue. ROI sensitivity controls vary marketing cost only; they do not re-estimate sales.
