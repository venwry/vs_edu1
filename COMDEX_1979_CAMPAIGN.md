# COMDEX 1979: More room to work. More freedom to choose.

Accepted campaign for the scenario's **December 3, 1979** launch. Aurelia+ is the new hardware flagship; Abacus 2.0 turns calculated values into charts and finished documents; the Compatibility Kit enables independent manufacturers. The canonical structured programme is [comdex_1979_campaign.json](comdex_1979_campaign.json). This is a campaign plan, not evidence of completed benchmarks, signed advertising contracts or qualified third-party products.

- **Aurelia+:** Your existing software. Less waiting.
- **Abacus 2.0:** Calculate once. Chart it. Print the documents.
- **Manufacturers:** Independently implement published interfaces to serve an established application ecosystem.

## The speed comparison

Allocate one minute to the controlled setup, two to the shaded cube, two to the business workload and one to results. Show both screens simultaneously and begin with a visible countdown. Both machines calculate rotation, projection, visible faces and shading, then draw each frame. There are no prerecorded or precomputed frames.

Use the same executable and data, resolution, shading method and rendering settings, with equivalent qualified displays. Show elapsed time and completed frames; run a fixed rotation/frame sequence with no silent skipping or reduced quality. Use a workload that fits both machines and show extra capacity separately. Disclose unavoidable configuration differences. Both machines must finish correctly.

The application comparison uses the same Abacus 2.0 executable, worksheet, calculation settings and chart workload. Time recalculation and chart generation, excluding physical printer time. Animation measures the combined calculation/display path, not pure CPU speed. Catalog clocks of 4 and 6 MHz do not establish a 1.5x application speedup. Publish no speed multiplier until measured; retain test configurations and results in the press pack.

## Abacus 2.0: calculations into documents

Use fictitious employee data: one row per employee with name, department, pay components, deductions and precalculated net pay.

1. Inspect the values and reconcile totals.
2. Show payroll costs by department with the catalogued text-mode vertical or horizontal bar charts.
3. Open one reusable payslip layout and identify fields linked to spreadsheet values.
4. Preview different employees to demonstrate field substitution.
5. Print the complete batch, one correct payslip per selected employee.

Validate field mapping, missing values, rounding, pagination, batch completeness and save/reopen before the show. The head of independent BPI then demonstrates the source payroll workflow and documented versioned disk-file exchange. Reconcile source and imported totals. No live database link or direct ledger write-back is implied.

Abacus formats and prints supplied calculations; payroll rules and statutory updates remain with the payroll application or maintained worksheet. Sparse storage supports useful workloads and remains an implementation advantage rather than a permanently exclusive general technique. The shaded-cube demonstration is a separate utility, not a promise of new Abacus graphical chart types.

Promote Abacus 2.0 to owners of **both Aurelia and Aurelia+** in supported configurations. Aurelia+ demonstrates less waiting and greater capacity without making the older computer obsolete.

## Offers and industry policy

New-buyer quotations identify hardware, software and printing configuration. Independent BPI licences and specialist setup are separate. Existing-owner guides explain supported upgrades and workloads that justify Aurelia+. Prioritise training, demonstrations and implementation help over another general price cut.

Manufacturers receive freely accessible specifications and self-test requirements, a physical kit, optional bounded engineering support and optional configuration-specific certification. A bespoke or exclusive partnership is unnecessary. Native displays and peripherals remain valid through adapters; FlexiBUS and Logix video are separate profiles. Software and firmware redistribution requires the relevant licences. See [AURELIA_COMPATIBILITY_KIT.md](AURELIA_COMPATIBILITY_KIT.md).

Logix focuses new hardware development and promotion on Aurelia+ while enabling independent Aurelia-class clones and maintaining installed-base support. No immediate withdrawal date is invented.
