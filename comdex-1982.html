Professional offering

  • Host: Artemis PRO, 1 MiB reference configuration, AIONix and qualified disk storage; final application minimum measured with representative drawings.
  • Storage: Standard 5 MB disk is not promised sufficient for every CAD workload. Quote a tested storage/backup configuration; do not invent a larger shipping accessory.
  • Professional display: 128 KiB professional card with separate 1 KiB sprite SRAM and Ball HD17H-based analog Mono Pro 17; use dated revision effective 1982-11-29.
  • Secondary display: COMDEX reference station includes the existing 64 KiB card and Color Pro 14 for commands/status. Upgrade buyers may retain a compatible existing primary card/display.
  • Software: One separately identified Autodesk AutoCAD 1.0 Artemis/AIONix licence. Do not imply Logix ownership, a free licence, or later AutoCAD features.
  • Pointing: One Logix Mouse; typed coordinates remain available for numerical precision. Explicit Autodesk mouse and dual-display integration.
  • Output: Demonstrate a qualified pen plotter with a documented driver; plotter is optional and separately quoted. No unselected model is represented as bundled.

November graphics revision

128 KiB display RAM plus separate 1 KiB sprite SRAM. Five legacy sprites, inherited text/bitmap modes, rectangle blitter, clipped fills and antialiased line engine. Additional card uses a general Artemis expansion slot; the colour card retains its dedicated slot.

The reserved 1536-byte list at 0x1FA00–0x1FFFF contains up to 512 three-byte commands: 8-bit relative output-scanline offset, 4-bit register and 12-bit RGB. Explicit count, frame reset, long-gap handling and per-line write budget prevent timing ambiguity. Static palette is the CAD default.

Logix Mono Pro 17

Ball HD-series HD17H, 17-inch horizontal-format OEM assembly, analog/linear-video option. Logix digital pixel index -> AION palette/controller -> 4-bit intensity DAC -> level-matched Ball analog input and linear amplifier. TTL/ECL-only input is not sufficient.

16 available intensity levels; four simultaneous shades in 800×600×2bpp, sixteen in 640×200×4bpp. Target 800×600 progressive at 60 Hz requires a qualified 37.5 kHz deflection revision. Older modes are centered in the native raster unless another timing is explicitly supported. The analog input option is documented; it is preferable to the binary TTL/ECL option for grayscale.

Proposed campaign

A clear view of your next design.
Bring a drawing. See it on Artemis. Take home the plot.

  • See the drawing clearly: Native 800x600 drawing area on the 17-inch screen, four shades selected from 16 levels, static CAD palette and demonstrated line quality.
  • Keep commands and drawing in view: Colour command/status display alongside the dedicated drawing screen, with explicit keyboard/mouse focus.
  • Take the drawing beyond the screen: Save, reopen, exchange and plot the same test drawing; verify coordinates and output scale.
  • Buy an application with a supported platform: Autodesk application support and Logix hardware/integration support, qualified configurations, dealer training and a clear upgrade route.

12-minute live demonstration

  • 0-2 minutes: Open a representative mechanical or floor-plan drawing from disk; show the command screen and drawing screen together.
  • 2-5 minutes: Create and edit supported lines/arcs using typed coordinates and mouse input. Use only commands present in the qualified AutoCAD 1.0 build.
  • 5-7 minutes: Pan/zoom and compare ordinary versus antialiased line rendering at the same scale. State measured redraw behaviour; do not promise arbitrary real-time performance.
  • 7-9 minutes: Save and reopen the drawing, demonstrate tested exchange, and show the resulting plot.
  • 9-12 minutes: Explain complete-system and existing-owner upgrade routes, support ownership, and book an evaluation.

Run a small live plot; disclose any larger pre-plotted sample as such. Do not imply a long plot completes within a short demonstration.

Schedule and follow-up

  • 1982-09: Use the existing Autodesk beta programme for reference workflows and qualified customer feedback; no second overlapping beta event.
  • 1982-10: Train selected dealers, rehearse the demo, confirm the plotter driver and prepare press materials.
  • 1982-11 before COMDEX: Invite technical press and qualified prospects; offer scheduled demos and customer-drawing evaluations.
  • 1982-11-29: Joint presentation, supervised trials and dealer meetings; accept orders only against explicit configuration and delivery terms.
  • 1982-12: Telephone/postal follow-up within five business days, regional evaluations, delivery acceptance and early-customer training.
  • 1983-01 to 1983-02: Publish approved customer case studies and measured support findings; review conversion and product defects.

Campaign allocation

Proposal allocation only; dollar envelope to be costed after booth, staffing, hardware and Autodesk co-marketing terms are agreed.

  • Booth and live demo: 30%
  • Demo equipment shipping and spares: 15%
  • Staff travel and dealer training: 20%
  • Press materials and customer invitations: 10%
  • Regional evaluations and followup: 15%
  • Contingency: 10%

Separate from the $253000 NCC campaign provision. Credit shared staff and reused demo equipment; do not charge capital twice or invent sales uplift. Team Edition adoption subsidy remains separate unit economics.

Measure outcomes

  • Qualified demonstrations with named organisation, workload and next step
  • Evaluations completed and customer acceptance findings
  • Written quotes and conversion to delivered, accepted installations
  • Dealers trained and reference configurations qualified
  • Support workload, defects, file-exchange problems and plot accuracy

Acceptance and support

  • Run the agreed release-quality native AutoCAD build on the reference configuration
  • Load/save and exchange a representative drawing; verify numerical geometry
  • Use both displays and the mouse with correct focus and cursor restoration
  • Show readable antialiased lines and stable display refresh under accelerator load
  • Plot a physical drawing and verify scale and geometry
  • Recover from interrupted work and restore a backup
  • Document supported features; no automatic later-version dimensioning, object-snap or 3D claims
  • Logix: Hardware, AIONix integration, drivers, installation and dealer coordination.
  • Autodesk: AutoCAD licence, application support and drawing-file compatibility.
  • Joint: Reference configuration testing, dealer training and escalation; define response commitments before quoting service.
