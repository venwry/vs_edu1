What these shares mean

Worldwide shipment-unit share, including home and business microcomputers. Peripherals, software, upgrades and installed-base totals are excluded. Clone rows count compatible product lines only; their unrelated legacy machines remain in “Other”. Aion Tech UK shipments are already included in Logix. For the all-computer view, 1981 and 1982 market sizes are calibrated to contemporary estimates reported by TIME. The 1980 and 1983 totals and all quarterly allocations are planning assumptions. These are alternate-history scenarios, not historical vendor market-share measurements. Only 20% of platform demand expands the baseline market; the remainder displaces other suppliers.

Contracts do not guarantee shipment volumes. North Star and Vector prospect forecasts are replaced, never added to these contracted-partner estimates. SGS chip production is not a computer shipment.
