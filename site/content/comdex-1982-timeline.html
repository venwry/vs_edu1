Proposed campaign

A clear view of your next design.
Bring a drawing. See it on Artemis. Take home the plot.

  • See the drawing clearly: Native 800x600 drawing area on the 17-inch screen, four shades selected from 16 levels, static CAD palette and demonstrated line quality.
  • Keep commands and drawing in view: Colour command/status display alongside the dedicated drawing screen, with explicit keyboard/mouse focus.
  • Take the drawing beyond the screen: Save, reopen, exchange and plot the same test drawing; verify coordinates and output scale.
  • Buy an application with a supported platform: Autodesk application support and Logix hardware/integration support, qualified configurations, dealer training and a clear upgrade route.

12-minute live demonstration

  • 0-2 minutes: Open a representative mechanical or floor-plan drawing from disk; show the command screen and drawing screen together.
  • 2-5 minutes: Create and edit supported lines/arcs using typed coordinates and mouse input. Use only commands present in the qualified AutoCAD 1.0 build.
  • 5-7 minutes: Pan/zoom and compare ordinary versus antialiased line rendering at the same scale. State measured redraw behaviour; do not promise arbitrary real-time performance.
  • 7-9 minutes: Save and reopen the drawing, demonstrate tested exchange, and show the resulting plot.
  • 9-12 minutes: Explain complete-system and existing-owner upgrade routes, support ownership, and book an evaluation.

Run a small live plot; disclose any larger pre-plotted sample as such. Do not imply a long plot completes within a short demonstration.

Schedule and follow-up

  • 1982-09: Use the existing Autodesk beta programme for reference workflows and qualified customer feedback; no second overlapping beta event.
  • 1982-10: Train selected dealers, rehearse the demo, confirm the plotter driver and prepare press materials.
  • 1982-11 before COMDEX: Invite technical press and qualified prospects; offer scheduled demos and customer-drawing evaluations.
  • 1982-11-29: Joint presentation, supervised trials and dealer meetings; accept orders only against explicit configuration and delivery terms.
  • 1982-12: Telephone/postal follow-up within five business days, regional evaluations, delivery acceptance and early-customer training.
  • 1983-01 to 1983-02: Publish approved customer case studies and measured support findings; review conversion and product defects.

Campaign allocation

Proposal allocation only; dollar envelope to be costed after booth, staffing, hardware and Autodesk co-marketing terms are agreed.

  • Booth and live demo: 30%
  • Demo equipment shipping and spares: 15%
  • Staff travel and dealer training: 20%
  • Press materials and customer invitations: 10%
  • Regional evaluations and followup: 15%
  • Contingency: 10%

Separate from the $253000 NCC campaign provision. Credit shared staff and reused demo equipment; do not charge capital twice or invent sales uplift. Team Edition adoption subsidy remains separate unit economics.

Measure outcomes

  • Qualified demonstrations with named organisation, workload and next step
  • Evaluations completed and customer acceptance findings
  • Written quotes and conversion to delivered, accepted installations
  • Dealers trained and reference configurations qualified
  • Support workload, defects, file-exchange problems and plot accuracy
