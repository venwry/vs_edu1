Pointy ideas with a solid point.

Introducing the Logix Mouse. Point, select, and get to work.

June colour/mouse offering and around-June professional hardware availability. AutoCAD is a November COMDEX development target, not a June shipping application.

June offering

Color Pro 14 and the 64 KiB card add 640×400 at 2 bpp and 640×200 at 4 bpp, retaining previous functions and the bounded scanline palette list. Logix Mouse has two buttons and two rollers. CP/M and AIONix TUI updates support standard controls; Entertainment Suite teaches pointing through Sudoku, Solitaire, Spider and FreeCell.

The professional extension supplies 128 KiB, 800×600 at 2 bpp, a 2D blitter/line engine and Mono Pro 17. It displays four shades selected from 16 grayscale levels. June hardware availability supports Autodesk's November target.

CP/M and AIONix TUI mouse updates carry no new licence fee for existing licensed users; fulfillment media ships with mouse/upgrade kits (costed in mouse pack). Standalone replacement media and installation are quoted separately. Replaceable TUI services can update existing binaries; static libraries require relinking, custom drawing requires adaptation.

Presentation and booth

  • Open a familiar Abacus worksheet and edit with keyboard.
  • Use the mouse to select a field, activate a button and scroll with both rollers.
  • Show a colour report with labels/patterns preserving monochrome meaning.
  • Demonstrate a supported older application and explain its exact update path.
  • Show the colour upgrade for an existing compatible Artemis.
  • Close with qualified 800x600 professional graphics and the Autodesk November target.

Make your first point. — the hands-on invitation.

  • Try it yourself: Two-minute mouse tutorial, then Solitaire/FreeCell and the same actions in a business application. Book an Academy/dealer demonstration.
  • Your business at work: Abacus and supported business applications with realistic data. Configured quotation or upgrade order.
  • Professional graphics: Dual displays and engineering line/curve tests; distinguish Logix diagnostics from an actual AutoCAD preview. Professional evaluation / November AutoCAD invitation.
  • One platform, several suppliers: Named partner systems and explicitly tested application configurations. Developer, manufacturer and dealer meetings.
