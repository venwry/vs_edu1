# Artemis at NCC 1980

**Artemis. A new generation. A familiar way to work.**

Accepted alternative-history campaign for May 19, 1980. The structured source is [ncc_1980_campaign.json](ncc_1980_campaign.json). Architecture disclosure begins at this launch; 1979 public material remains unchanged.

## Presentation: 55 minutes plus questions

- 0–4 minutes: Adam: the business task and familiar way to work.
- 4–14 minutes: AIONix multitasking: edit, calculate and switch using familiar TUI conventions.
- 14–23 minutes: Native Business Tools and Abacus 2 charts/document fields; then independent BPI head demonstrates accounting.
- 23–30 minutes: One maintained C project, separate ERA and Z80 builds; optional compatibility card runs qualified unchanged legacy binaries.
- 30–38 minutes: Aurelia+, PDP-11/34 and Artemis: verified calculation benchmark, then visible animation.
- 38–42 minutes: Reveal: 32-bit RISC ERA 1 developed by AION.
- 42–47 minutes: Publish the compatibility commitment through 2000 and its precise scope.
- 47–51 minutes: Partners: independent BPI head, Digital Research and qualified device/software partners.
- 51–55 minutes: Prices, qualified configurations, order routes, developer kits and training.

## Claims and demonstration boundaries

**Memory.** Beyond the 64 KB application barrier; demonstrate a measured resident workload on a named 128 KB or 256 KB configuration. Finite RAM and process limits remain; no demand paging promised.

**Os.** Professional multitasking AIONix with Unix V7-inspired behaviour and familiar Logix TUI/keyboard conventions. Do not claim a Unix source licence or trademark endorsement without an established agreement.

**Compiler.** Artemis-hosted AION C IDE supplies ERA native and Z80 cross-compilers, target libraries and separate executables. Portable code following guidelines can require small changes; quantify changes for each demonstration rather than promising zero work.

**Applications.** Native Abacus 2, Business Tools and qualified independent BPI editions. Pulse 1 remains Aurelia+ software; a card demonstration requires guest OS/memory qualification. Native Pulse 3 and Abacus 3 launch only at COMDEX on November 19.

**Continuity.** Aurelia+ remains a supported value option; compatible independent 8-bit computers grow the ecosystem. No forced migration or promise that cheaper 8-bit demand disappears.

## Compatibility through 2000

Released Artemis application binaries using documented, supported ERA/AIONix interfaces retain a supported execution path on subsequent Logix professional platforms through 2000: native execution, a maintained compatibility runtime, or emulation. Source portability is a separate commitment, not a substitute for the binary execution path.

Undocumented hardware access, unqualified peripherals and unsupported interfaces; no promise of unchanged speed, unlimited memory, or every future device. Z80 programs use their separately qualified compatibility path.

Publish versioned ABI/API eligibility and conformance tests; preserve reference binaries and regression workloads; budget compatibility engineering, runtime distribution and support. Staffing, funding and validation remain explicit launch readiness gates.

Official scenario commitment at NCC 1980; future fulfilment is not established evidence.

## Performance demonstration

Identical vector transformations and fixed-point precision, inputs, rounding and verified output; time computation separately from rendering and I/O. Publish source, compiler options, configuration and repeat-run range.

Same shaded rotating cube, geometry and image quality; identify display hardware, frame-buffer and display-transfer costs separately. Do not infer pure CPU speed from unequal graphics systems. No performance ratios are approved before measurement.

## Campaign activities

- **Before NCC 1980:** Private partner qualification, dealer rehearsal, porting clinic pilots and embargoed briefings; public teaser promises a familiar workflow without revealing architecture or word size.
- **NCC 1980, May 19:** Live 55-minute keynote, independent BPI appearance after Abacus, benchmark method/results if measured, compatibility policy, software/configuration matrix and ordering sheets.
- **May–June 1980 after launch:** Dealer demonstrations and customer workflow trials; press technical briefing; Academy transition classes and developer migration clinics; maintain issue/qualification register.
- **July–October 1980:** Regional dealer roadshows, independent developer/OEM certification workshops, customer case studies with consent and measured results; review conversion, delivery backlog, porting effort and support capacity monthly.
- **COMDEX 1980, November 19:** Demonstrate released Abacus 3 and Pulse 3 on Artemis, report compatibility progress and retain Aurelia maintenance messaging. No pre-release revenue in the November 18 forecast.
