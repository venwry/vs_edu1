# Team Edition launch and June 15, 1982 pricing

AION Development System — Team Edition enters the offering in November 1981. November 19 is the project’s selected scenario date, not a sourced historical opening date.

## Offering

- One Artemis PRO server entitlement and four concurrent developer sessions; hardware and networking sold separately.
- AIONix Server adds default users, groups, authentication, project permissions, quotas and administration in its November revision.
- Repository: atomic multi-file commits, branches, release labels, comments, diffs and rollback; isolated workspaces and revision-stable references.
- Collaboration: text channels and posts referencing files/revisions; shared meetings, calendar, tasks, ownership and milestones.
- C Development Environment 2 loads over the network and runs locally on Artemis, Aurelia and Aurelia+. Compact Z80 clients use overlays and queue heavy/ERA builds to the server.
- CP/M/Z80 and AIONix/ERA are initial targets. Shared C and Breakthru TUI contracts reduce porting work; target-specific adapters, libraries and testing remain necessary. Apple and Commodore are roadmap targets only.
- Recommended server: 1 MiB Artemis PRO. Its existing 5 MB disk supports bounded pilot projects, not an unlimited complex-product repository. Rotate floppy backups and archive history. Larger qualified storage is a prerequisite for substantial deployments and is not included or priced as an invented shipping accessory.
- Four developers is a qualification target. Test recovery, access controls, concurrent edits, conflicts, backup restoration and both target builds before claiming production robustness.

## 1982 pricing policy and limitations

For recurring cost C, fee f and comparator B, use P = √(C×B/(1−f)): contribution/net revenue equals the percentage discount below B. If break-even exceeds B, use break-even and disclose the premium. Without a comparator, P = C/((1−f)×0.75), giving 25% contribution on net revenue. Prices below use the normal 25% ComputerLand fee, except existing direct-service rows at 0%.

1982 comparator amounts are explicitly **unverified nominal carry-forwards**, not observed 1982 prices. Costs are June 15 planning estimates: $8.30/h base wage, $0.047/kWh power, overhead index 1.43 and itemized OEM-price assumptions. The future market review should replace comparator estimates before treating these as final competitive prices.

Team Edition costs **$838.38** per package, including 12 disks, 1,000 pages, 20 onboarding staff-hours and 12 first-year support staff-hours. Its no-proxy price is **$1,490.45**, with hardware extra. No NRE or marketing amortization is hidden in this recurring cost; the price is not evidence of development-cost recovery. Client/server edition rows are included distribution components, not separately priced licenses. Additional seats and paid renewals remain unpriced roadmap items.
