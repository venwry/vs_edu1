## Assumptions and limits

**Period:** May 19–November 18, 1980 inclusive. May has 13/31 exposure; November 18/30. Earlier forecasts are unchanged. COMDEX launch-day Abacus 3/Pulse 3 revenue is excluded.

**Baseline:** April 1980 pre-price-response demand, from the prior model, is the full-month anchor. Apply run-rate growth and net campaign/competition effect once; then divide one buyer pool between all three computers. Artemis substitution is not added again as extra demand.

**Net Effect:** Judgement net uplift for launch/dealer confidence after competition and migration hesitation; not measured conversion. No separate speed, Academy or year-2000 bonus.

**Price Response:** Remaining intentions select Aurelia+. Each cohort responds to its package price relative to the prior Plus basket (old Aurelia uses its own basket). Premium-deterrence leaves the market; no automatic substitution. Existing products keep prior prices in May, then use June 1980 snapshots. Artemis May price is explicitly a provisional June-snapshot proxy. Price declines increase intentions according to the scenario elasticity, not by an additional marketing factor.

**Capacity:** Assumed deliveries, not proven production capacity. Separate Artemis ramp and model queues; 75% retry and 25% lapse at each reporting-period transition, including the split May transition. No reallocation between models.

**Accounting:** Computer package receipts combine Logix/AION after channel fees; contribution excludes overhead, campaign, compatibility maintenance, R&D, tax and financing. BPI is independent and excluded. Migration bundle already contains the compatibility card: never attach it again. Native Abacus 2 is included in Office/Migration bundles. Standalone upgrades, extra Pulse licences, SDK subscriptions and Academy tuition are excluded from this extension rather than treated as zero-demand products.

**Limits:** US planning scenarios, not orders or market-share estimates. Academy numerical horizon remains May 18; post-launch training is a campaign activity without a new enrolment projection. No COMDEX 1980 launch-day software receipts.

The April demand anchor is split into mutually exclusive intentions; central Artemis preference rises from 12% to 40%, before price response. Aurelia preference falls from 10% to 4%; Plus receives the remainder. Central assumed monthly Artemis delivery capacity rises from 600 to 5,500 (partial periods scaled). Conservative/strong capacity is 75%/120% of central. These are explicit planning inputs requiring production and dealer validation.

The 1%/7%/12% net campaign assumptions are sensitivities, not empirical evidence. Clones and competitors reduce the net effect; no manufacturer adoption contract or market-coverage denominator is invented. Availability, memory qualification, service quality and the compatibility promise’s maintenance costs remain open gates.
