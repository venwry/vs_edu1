ERA1 and its 6/8 MHz variants remain 55 mm2 on the original 3.0 um Micralign process. No CPU shrink or speed-bin uplift.

Scenario assumptions, not measured factory yields. Manufacturing release requires retention, speed, repair coverage, matched-tool and board qualification. The cheapest qualified recipe wins; a smaller feature size is not automatically cheaper.

Production decision

For the June 1981 snapshot, repaired 3 µm RAM beats the 2.75 µm candidate after spare area, repair operations and wafer costs. AION 64K chips replace purchased chips in PRO modules after qualification. RAM capacity, bus width, CPU speed and graphics specifications remain unchanged. The original purchased-memory record remains a qualified contingency, without booked savings.

Costs and balanced retail prices

The existing balanced-percentages rule is retained: P = √(C × B / (1 − channel fee)). It equates margin as a percentage of net receipts with the discount from the market reference. Savings are not passed through dollar for dollar.
