# Aurelia sales under revised pricing

Current forecast after balancing contribution margins against competitor-proxy discounts, using the dedicated sprite SRAM and AION system-controller costs. This is an alternative-history planning scenario, not recorded sales. All periods and financial totals are available in [sales.html](sales.html) and [sales_forecast.json](sales_forecast.json). The current horizon is November 18, 1980; the last quarter/year are partial.

## Pricing and manufacturing policy

Match contribution margin on net receipts to the discount below the existing competitor proxy. No-proxy offers use a 25% margin; two monitor snapshots use break-even pricing above their proxies. No speculative demand uplift for new features is applied. Frozen pricing baselines are unchanged. Cost assumptions remain engineering estimates, not quotations.

## Demand and period boundaries

Demand uses the existing 22,000 baseline × price ratio raised to negative scenario elasticity, rounded to 1,000 and capped before dated competitor/partner effects. Elasticities and capacity are judgement assumptions. Calendar 1978 has no pre-launch sales; the launch-year proxy runs June 1978 through May 1979 and omits June 1–3. No future event is applied before its defined boundary.

## BPI and Academy

Central first-purchase BPI installations: 2,008 in 1978 and 5,480 through May 1979. Customer spending is $657,007.12; independent BPI receipts after channel fees are $492,755.35. These are not additional computer sales or Logix revenue.

BPI execution adds 1,200 central computer deliveries. Strong-case requested/delivered BPI gains are 1,800/1,800, with 0 deferred. Attachment remains 15/20/25%, rounded monthly; accounting-only, four-module and suite cohorts are mutually exclusive. Upgrade credits are not counted as extra first-purchase revenue.

Academy planning retains five launch centres, 8–10 by end-1978, 14–18 by NCC 1979, 40–45 rooms, 50–56 instructors and 10,000–13,000 first-year enrolments. Repeat learners are not extra hardware buyers. Expand against occupancy/waiting lists and qualified staffing.

## Later campaigns and verification

June–November demand uses May run rates, annual pricing, dated campaign/competition effects and bounded deliveries; returning backlog is not new demand. Aurelia+ starts after December 3, 1979. NCC/COMDEX 1980 extend the model with their original disclosure dates, product mix and separate Academy/BPI accounting. Older narrative campaign snapshots are historical analyses; current figures are the generated JSON and Sales page.
